# Tuvara / GlocalUnit — NDA Template (DRAFT — not yet reviewed by a lawyer)

**Status: draft for Tushar's review.** A generic starting point for anyone
who needs to see Tuvara's code, strategy documents, or pilot business data
before you're ready to make that public — a future developer, intern,
agent, or contractor. Not reviewed against UK, EU, or Bangladesh contract
law. Written in English as it's a document meant to be signed by a real
counterparty, not internal documentation.

## Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is made between:

**Disclosing Party:** GlocalUnit / Tushar Hossain ("GlocalUnit")
**Receiving Party:** [Name], [role — e.g. developer / intern / agent] ("Recipient")

**Date:** [date]

**1. Purpose**
GlocalUnit is developing Tuvara, a business tool for micro and solo
entrepreneurs. To carry out [describe the specific task — e.g. "front-end
development work", "pilot onboarding in Bangladesh"], Recipient needs
access to some or all of: Tuvara's source code, product strategy
documents, pilot business data, or other confidential materials
("Confidential Information").

**2. Recipient agrees to:**
- Use Confidential Information only for the purpose described above.
- Not copy, share, publish, or disclose Confidential Information to any
  third party without GlocalUnit's prior written consent.
- Not use Confidential Information to build, assist, or advise a
  competing product or service.
- Return or delete all Confidential Information upon request or when the
  purpose above is complete, whichever comes first.

**3. What is NOT covered**
This Agreement does not restrict information that: was already publicly
known through no fault of Recipient, was already lawfully known to
Recipient before this Agreement, or is independently developed by
Recipient without reference to Confidential Information.

**4. Duration**
This Agreement remains in effect for [e.g. 2 years] from the date above,
or until GlocalUnit releases the relevant Confidential Information
publicly, whichever is sooner.

**5. No employment/partnership implied**
Signing this Agreement does not, by itself, create an employment,
partnership, or any other ongoing relationship between the parties.
