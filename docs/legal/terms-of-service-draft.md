# Tuvara — Terms of Use (DRAFT — not yet reviewed by a lawyer or shown to any pilot)

**Status: draft for Tushar's review.** This is a short, plain-language
starting point, not a finished legal document. Nothing here has been
checked against UK, EU, or Bangladesh consumer/contract law, and it should

## Tuvara — Terms of Use

_Last updated: [date]_

Tuvara ("the app", "the service") is provided by GlocalUnit ("we", "us").
By using Tuvara, you agree to the following terms.

**1. What Tuvara is**
Tuvara is a tool to help you catalogue stock, get pricing suggestions, and
generate social-media listing text for your own business. Every suggestion
the app makes — prices, categories, listing text — is a suggestion, not a
decision made for you. You are always the one who decides what to charge,
what to post, and what to do with your own stock and customer information.

**2. Your data**
In its current form, Tuvara stores your business data (stock, sales,
customers) only on your own device, in your browser. We do not operate a
central database of your data and cannot see it unless you choose to share
it with us directly (for example, by sending us a screenshot for support).
If this changes in a future version — for example, if data starts being
stored on a shared server — we will tell you before it happens, not after.

**3. No copying, reverse engineering, or re-use of the service**
You may use Tuvara for your own business. You may not copy, reproduce,
decompile, reverse engineer, extract, or otherwise attempt to recreate the
app's source code, pricing logic, or listing-text generation, or use it to
build a competing product or service, without our prior written
permission.

**4. No guarantees**
Tuvara is provided as-is, in active early development ("pilot" stage). We
do not guarantee it will be free of bugs, available at all times, or
suitable for any particular purpose. Pricing suggestions are estimates
based on the numbers you enter — you're responsible for checking they make
sense for your business before you use them.

**5. No payment processing**
Tuvara does not process payments, hold your money, or connect to your bank
account. Any prices, totals, or "amount owed" figures shown in the app are
for your own record-keeping only.

**6. Changes to these terms**
We may update these terms as Tuvara develops. We'll let pilot users know
directly (WhatsApp/email) if anything material changes, rather than
silently updating this page.

## Notes for Tushar (remove before publishing)

- Section 3 is the piece that actually connects to the "protect Tuvara"
  analysis — it's the plain-language version of "please don't copy this",
  sitting alongside the LICENSE file's more formal wording. It won't stop
  a determined copier technically, but it gives you something concrete to
  point to if it's ever needed.
- Section 2's "we do not operate a central database" is accurate today
  (client-only, localStorage) but will need updating the moment any part
  of Tuvara moves to a shared backend (see the GlasBox migration notes) —
  flagging this so it doesn't quietly become inaccurate later.
- No cookie/analytics clause included, because I didn't find any
  analytics or tracking in the codebase to describe. If that changes,
  this needs a clause too.
- Consider a matching, shorter Bangla version once this English draft is
  finalised — same discipline as the rest of the app's Bangla content
  (not yet reviewed by a native speaker).
